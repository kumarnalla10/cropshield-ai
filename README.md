# cropshield-ai

🌱 CropShield AI — Turning Field Conditions into Smarter Farm Decisions

The platform acts as a real-time bridge between field conditions and expert agronomic guidance. Farmers can simply upload a photo of an affected crop or leaf, provide their location, and receive an AI-generated advisory based on the detected crop condition and local weather conditions.

🔍 How it works

📸 1. Upload Crop Image

Farmers upload an image of a diseased or affected leaf. The AI analyzes the visible symptoms to identify the possible crop disease or issue.

📍 2. Share Location

The farmer's location is used to understand the local environmental conditions and retrieve relevant weather information.

🌦️ 3. Analyze Weather Conditions

Live and upcoming weather conditions such as rainfall, temperature, and humidity are considered before recommending treatment.

🤖 4. Get Actionable Advisory

CropShield AI converts all this information into a simple advisory explaining:

What is wrong with the crop
What action or treatment can be considered
When it is safer to take action
How upcoming weather may affect the treatment

🎯 The Vision

Instead of requiring farmers to interpret complex agricultural and weather information themselves, CropShield AI brings these insights together in one simple interface.
The ultimate goal is to support farmer livelihood, reduce avoidable crop losses, improve decision-making, and contribute to food security and climate-resilient agriculture.
